Course navigation studies

Two ways to stay oriented.

Both concepts keep the current ASL colors, typography, and calm spacing. They differ in how much course structure stays visible while you read.

Prototype A

Persistent course sidebar

The path outline, module groups, completion marks, and current lesson remain beside the reading area on wider screens. On mobile, the outline becomes a collapsible Course Content drawer.

Prototype B

Collapsible course navigation

The lesson gets more horizontal room. Course structure sits in a disclosure panel that learners open when they need to check their position or choose another lesson.

Inspect the responsive layouts

Open either prototype, then resize the browser window to approximately 1440px, 1024px, and 390px. Both pages use responsive CSS and can be opened directly without FastAPI.

At 1440px, compare the persistent outline with the wider reading area. At 1024px, check whether both columns remain comfortable. At 390px, check the course-content disclosure, lesson text, answer choices, and navigation controls.
